OUR STORY.

A Journey of Growth &
Enduring Achievement.

1986

Founded by Engineer Mohamed Bahaa El Din Abdalla, initiating specialized infrastructure contracting across Egypt.

1994

Joined the Egyptian Federation of Construction and Building Contractors with Grade A infrastructure classification.

1996

Successfully delivered the company's first major public water transmission and sanitation landmarks.

2000

Formally converted into a Joint Stock Company under the corporate name Al Bahaa Contracting S.A.E.

Today

Operating nationwide as Al Bahaa Construction, delivering mega infrastructure projects supporting Egypt's sustainable development agenda.
